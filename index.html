ElderCare Connect

Technology that
cares for elders.

We help families coordinate daily health routines, keeping elders safe and caregivers informed—behind the scenes, with zero friction.

Two sides, one bridge.

Great care is about more than just remembering a pill. It's about peace of mind, usability, reliability, and preserving dignity. Sanjeevani Setu is designed specifically for the unique needs of older adults who find modern apps overwhelming.

From the initial concept to the interface design, our focus is entirely on building a solution that is practical, accessible, and thoughtfully minimal. We strip away the complicated menus to deliver a clean, consent-first experience that adapts to the user, not the other way around.
